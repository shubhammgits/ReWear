## WebKit-specific findings
- **Production WebKit 27:** no console errors. Supports `overflow-x: clip`, `svh`/`dvh`, `backdrop-filter` (prefixed and unprefixed), sticky, `aspect-ratio` and `:has()`. The sticky `/browse` toolbar holds at `top: 80px`. Lenis and the custom cursor are correctly off on touch.
- **Dev build in WebKit only:** `SyntaxError: Unexpected EOF` in `app/layout.js`. This is a dev-server artifact and isn't present in production (`qa/before/webkit-hero-dev.jpg` vs `webkit-hero-prod.jpg`). It's the trigger for M10.
- **Older iOS (≤ 15)**, not testable with this WebKit build: no `overflow-x: clip` and no `svh`. Fallbacks are needed (M7 and every `100svh`).
- The C1–C3 closet issues reproduce in WebKit and Chromium alike. C4–C6 were flagged in Chromium; the WebKit 390/430 runs didn't flag C5. The same layout code applies, so they're fixed for both.
